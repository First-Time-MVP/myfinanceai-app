MyFinanceAI Ingestion

MyFinanceAI Ingestion is a private tool used by a single household to keep its own financial records organized. It is not offered to the public, and no one outside that household can sign up for it.

With each account holder's own permission, it reads that person's email read-only, so bills, statements and notices can be filed in the household's private, encrypted records. It never sends, deletes, moves or changes any email, and it shares nothing with anyone.

Each person who grants access can withdraw it at any time from their Google Account's Third-party connections page.
