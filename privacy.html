Privacy policy

MyFinanceAI Ingestion · effective October 7, 2026

Who uses it

MyFinanceAI Ingestion is a private tool used only by the members of one household, for their own financial records. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the permission of each account holder who grants it, the app reads that person's Gmail messages and their attachments, read-only (the Google permission "View your email messages and settings"). It never sends, deletes, moves, labels or changes any email.

How the information is used

Only to keep the household's own financial records: to find and file bills, statements and notices, and to answer the household's own questions about them. It is not used for advertising, not used to train any general-purpose artificial-intelligence model, and not used for anything other than this.

Where it is kept

In the household's own private records, encrypted, reachable only with credentials the household holds.

Sharing

None. The information is never sold, rented or shared with anyone, and no person outside the household reads it.

Google API Services

MyFinanceAI Ingestion's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Withdrawing access and deletion

Each person can withdraw the app's access at any time from their Google Account's Third-party connections page. A household member may ask for their information to be deleted by writing to the address below.
